🔒 Security & privacy — secure build v3

Your follower snapshots are encrypted in this browser with AES-GCM. Your passphrase is used to derive the key and is never stored. ZIP/JSON parsing happens on this device.

  • No Instagram password or session cookie is collected.
  • No analytics, ads, external APIs, or third-party scripts are included.
  • The page Content Security Policy blocks network connections from the app.
  • Encrypted backups stay encrypted until restored with their passphrase.
  • CSV and printable reports are intentionally plaintext exports; protect those files separately.

Possible block/unavailable signals are heuristics only. A missing account can also mean deactivation, deletion, username changes, or both sides changing follow state. Follower Lens never presents a block as confirmed.

Export Instagram data

In Instagram / Accounts Center, request a download of your information and choose JSON if available. Include Followers and following. Instagram's menu wording can change over time.

  1. Request your Instagram information in JSON format.
  2. Download the ZIP when Instagram makes it available.
  3. Import the ZIP directly here. If ZIP parsing isn't supported by your browser, extract it on your phone and select the follower/following JSON files instead.
  4. Import again later. The app compares the new snapshot with the previous one.

Follower Lens processes the export locally. Snapshot history is encrypted before being stored in IndexedDB. Clearing browser/site data can remove your history, so create an encrypted backup periodically.
